# Milevana Privacy Policy (summary)

**Effective date:** [date]  
**App:** Milevana, a free, ad-free vehicle expense and maintenance tracker (Android app and web app)  
**Responsible party:** Poovarasu Sekar, an individual developer  
**Contact:** support@poovarasu.dev

## What we collect

- **Account.** When you start the Android app, an anonymous account is created for you. If you link a Google account (or sign in with Google on the web), we receive your Google account name and email address.
- **Your vehicle data.** Everything you enter: vehicles (name, type, make, model, year, plate, odometer), fuel and charging, expenses, services, income, trips, notes, and your settings.
- **Crash diagnostics.** We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive anonymous crash and error reports. They contain document ids, error codes and app state. They never contain amounts, plates, names or notes. Crashlytics assigns an installation id to your app installation; we do not use your account id as the Crashlytics user id.

## What we do not do

- No advertising and no ad SDK. We do not sell your data. We do not use analytics. The app does not ask for your location.

## Where your data lives

Your data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase) [location] and, on Android, in an on-device cache so the app works offline. Data is encrypted in transit.

## Google Drive

If you use backups or receipts, the app asks for access to a folder it creates in your own Google Drive (the `drive.file` scope). It cannot see your other files. Backups and receipts are stored in your Drive, not on our servers.

In-app deletion arrives in a later version and this policy will be updated then.

## Children

Milevana is for people aged 18 and over.

## Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the app changes. Questions: support@poovarasu.dev.
