# Milevana v1 — Consolidated Decision Register

**Decisions:** 104, from the eight backlogs · **Date:** 2026-10-07 · **14 answered by the owner, 90 assumed**

## What this is

Every `[DECISION REQUIRED]` item from the eight implementation backlogs in one place, read directly from those files so the wording matches. The register organizes them and records how each was settled (answered by the owner, or assumed so work can proceed): by the order they block work (section 1), by theme (section 2), and by the answers that settle several at once (section 3). Section 4 is the full list with the answer or assumption for each.

**IDs.** `DR` Foundation, `LD` Logging, `RD` Reminders, `PD` Reports and dashboard, `ED` Drive and export, `SD` Sharing, `CD` Checklists, `VD` Release. They are tracking labels, not requirement IDs. Each ID is defined in the backlog named in its row.

**Level** is the one each backlog assigned: **Schema** or **Schema/rules** = fixes stored data or rules, needed before data in the final shape is written; **Story** = needed before the named story is accepted; **Copy** = wording; **Input** = content, assets or an external answer.

## 0c. Assumptions (all 90)

In answer order, then by ID. Section 4 repeats each with its full question.

| ID | Decision | Assumption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LD-03** | Amount and input rules | Amounts must be greater than zero, except a service total that floors at zero (Appendix E). Amounts have at most 12 digits and the currency's own decimals; quantity has at most 3 decimals. Derived values: total is price times quantity rounded half-up to the minor unit; price per unit is total divided by quantity rounded half-up to the milli-unit; quantity is total divided by price rounded to 3 decimals; trip reimbursement is distance times rate rounded half-up to the minor unit. The two fields the user edited most recently count as entered; the third is recalculated. |
| **LD-06** | "Other" free text and category storage | The typed 'Other' text is stored in `category` itself (category holds the name text, per DR-03). For service lines, `serviceType` is `other` with the text in `otherLabel`; a service entry whose own category is 'Other' stores the typed text in `category`. Fixed categories store the English names from the SRS lists. |
| **LD-07** | Income and expense `details` | Income `details` is `{ platform?: string }` (short text, 60). Expense has no `details`. This adds an income details shape to Appendix E. |
| **RD-05** | Roll-forward and Mark done | Months are added to the completing entry's date, clamping to the last day of the month. If the completing entry has no odometer the odometer trigger rolls forward from the vehicle's `lastOdometer`. A repeating reminder stays `active` after roll-forward and records `completedAt` and `completedByEntryId` of the latest completion (overwriting earlier ones); a one-off becomes `done`. Mark done on a repeating reminder rolls forward from today and `lastOdometer`; on a one-off it becomes done. |
| **ED-04** | Backup contents | Settings included: currency, distance unit, volume unit, efficiency format, date format, theme, locale and backup frequency (not the mute list, `drive` fields or `deletion`). Entries keep `attachments[]` (with `driveFileId`) and `uploaderUids`, with `sharedWithUids` emptied. `formatVersion` is the integer 1. `app` holds the name 'Milevana' and the installed app version. |
| **ED-07** | Attachment details | `status` values: `queued`, `uploading`, `uploaded`, `failed`, `missing`. Transitions: queued to uploading to uploaded or failed; failed back to queued on retry; uploaded to missing when Drive reports the file gone. Photos are output as JPEG at quality 80 with a 1600 px long edge from jpeg, png or heic input; documents are PDF only (up to 5 MB). Drive file name `<entry date YYYY-MM-DD>_<entry type>_<attachment id>.<ext>`. `sizeBytes` is the stored size. Orientation is applied to the pixels and metadata is stripped. |
| **SD-01** | Reading invites and guessing | Rules allow any signed-in user, anonymous included, to `get` an invite by its exact code (so the join screen can show names before linking). Listing is denied except the owner's query by `ownerId`. No guard beyond the 8-character alphabet (accepted limitation, OQ-33). |
| **SD-03** | Join write and validity | The join writes `members[uid] = { role: 'member', name, email, joinedAt: server time, inviteCode }` from the joiner's Google profile and sets `usedBy` and `usedAt` on the invite. Rules require a non-anonymous sign-in for joining. An archived vehicle cannot be joined (the invite is treated as invalid). An invite for a deleted vehicle shows 'This vehicle no longer exists'. Join needs the network; the button is disabled offline. |
| **SD-06** | Leave, remove and transfer write shapes | Leave: a member's write removes only their own uid from `memberIds` and `members`. Remove: the owner removes another uid from both. Transfer: the owner's write sets `ownerId` to an existing member and swaps the roles in `members` in the same write, with no acceptance. Rules deny the owner removing themselves. The transfer adversarial tests try an `ownerId` change to a uid not in `memberIds`, and a change without the role swap; both must fail. |
| **CD-01** | Template constraints | Template name 1 to 60 characters, unique per vehicle ignoring case (app-side). Item label and group 1 to 60 characters; groups are free text and items display under their group in order of first use. At least 1 item. Items can be reordered. Duplicate labels are allowed. |
| **CD-03** | Item ids and concurrent edits | Snapshot item ids equal the template item ids. Template item ids are random ids generated when the item is added. Two members editing one template at once: the last write wins. A run in progress is unaffected by template edits because it uses the copy loaded at its start. |
| **DR-04** | The 12-colour palette | Twelve palette ids `c01` to `c12`, each a distinct Material 3-compatible colour chosen at implementation and contrast-checked in light and dark mode. Vehicle-type icons come from the Material icon set that React Native Paper already uses: car, motorbike, scooter, truck, bus, and a generic vehicle icon for 'other'. (Basis: palette values are not a requirement; ids are stable per DR-03.) |
| **DR-07** | Currency and number formatting | Selectable currencies: active ISO 4217 codes with a minor-unit exponent of 0, 2 or 3 (funds, metals and test codes such as XAU, XTS, XXX are excluded). Amounts show the symbol before the number with a `.` decimal separator; INR uses Indian digit grouping (12,34,567.00), other currencies use international grouping. `mpg` follows the Volume setting (UK gallons when the setting is UK gallons, otherwise US gallons). Efficiency, distance and volume settings are independent and any combination is allowed. Converted distance and volume show one decimal; efficiency shows one decimal. (Basis: Intl number formatting; avoids constraining settings the SRS leaves free.) |
| **DR-08** | What Foundation displays for AC-F5 | Settings-driven values are shown on screens that already exist: vehicle rows show the odometer converted from the vehicle's unit to the Distance unit setting (km and mi convert; `hours` stays in hours), vehicle detail shows the creation date in the Date format setting, and the Settings screen shows a small read-only preview line for currency, volume, efficiency and date (for example an example amount, volume and efficiency). The preview is how AC-F5's currency, volume and efficiency display is verified before entries exist. |
| **DR-09** | Device-local UI flags | Welcome screen: shown once per uid until Get started is tapped; the flag is stored locally keyed by uid. If the app is closed before a vehicle is added, relaunch goes straight to 'Add your first vehicle'. A new anonymous uid after sign-out shows the welcome again. Link prompt: the dismissed flag is stored locally keyed by uid; the prompt shows when the cross-vehicle entry count is 5 or more, the user is not linked and has not dismissed it; later deletes do not reset a dismissal. |
| **DR-10** | Conflict-prompt gaps | When the phone has nothing to move (0 vehicles and 0 entries) the three-way prompt is replaced by a two-button dialog, 'This Google account already has data. Switch to it?', with Switch and Cancel. Counts use plural forms (1 vehicle, 2 vehicles; 1 entry, 14 entries). Discard on 'Finish moving your data' asks 'Discard the data waiting to move? | **DR-18** | Web offline details | A web save not confirmed within 15 seconds is treated as failed: the offline banner shows and the form stays open with Retry. A blocked popup is detected when the popup sign-in rejects with the popup-blocked or popup-closed-by-browser error (not a user cancel), and the redirect flow is then started. |
| **DR-19** | Sync issues semantics | Retry resubmits the original queued change unchanged. Discard drops the local change so the item returns to the server state. The Sync issues list is kept for the running session only; it shows the kind of item and its name, never amounts, plates or notes. |
| **DR-23** | Operations exposed by `reminderRepo`, `templateRepo`, `inviteRepo` in Foundation | Foundation exposes validated read and subscribe for `reminderRepo`, `templateRepo` and `inviteRepo`. Each later sub-project defines and tests its own write operations (`REM-05`, `CHK-04`, `SHR-06`). `inviteRepo` is schema and skeleton only in Foundation. |
| **LD-01** | Mileage definition and display | Mileage for a full fill is the distance since the previous full fill of the same fuel divided by the total quantity of that fuel added since then (the later full fill plus any partial fills in between). If the later fill or any fill in between has `missedPrevious` on, no mileage is computed for that interval. Rounded to one decimal. Shown on the fuel entry's History row (and in a summary line after saving). The first fill of a fuel has no mileage. A bi-fuel vehicle shows per-fuel mileage on rows; cost per km stays a Dashboard and Reports figure. `hours` vehicles show none. |
| **LD-02** | Fuel form units and defaults | Petrol, diesel and LPG quantities follow the Volume setting (litres, US gallons, UK gallons) with a per-entry unit picker; CNG is kg; electric is kWh. LPG efficiency follows the Efficiency setting like petrol. The default fuel type is the vehicle's first fuel type, then the one last used. 'Missed previous fill-up' defaults to off. 'Full tank' is labelled 'Full charge' for an electric fuel type. |
| **LD-04** | Entry date | New entries default to the current local date and time, to the minute. Future dates are allowed, with a small 'future date' note and no maximum. |
| **LD-05** | Odometer sanity check | The odometer check compares every entry that has an odometer, including a trip's start and end, ordered by entry date then creation time. The warning shows inline under the field ('Lower than your reading of X on <date>') and Save stays enabled. A trip stores its end odometer as the entry's `odometer` and updates `lastOdometer` (maximum); its start odometer counts as a reading for the check and for reports but does not update `lastOdometer`. |
| **LD-08** | Service constraints | A service has 1 to 20 lines. Zero-cost lines are allowed. The entry category is chosen freely from the Services list and need not match any line. |
| **LD-09** | Trip validation | Required: start and end date and time, start and end odometer, and purpose. From and to are optional. End must not be before start and end odometer must not be below start (hard validation, since distance would be negative). Trips on `hours` vehicles are allowed, with distance shown in hours and the rate per hour. Times use the device time zone. |
| **LD-10** | History loading and rows | History loads all of a vehicle's entries (cache-first) and renders them in a virtualized list; no paging in v1. A row shows the type icon, date, category or fuel summary, amount, the 'Logged by' name for others' entries, the odometer if present, and the sync icon. Rows are grouped under month headers. |
| **LD-11** | Filters and search controls | Type filter: multi-select chips, default all. Date range: start and end date pickers, default none. Filters do not persist across launches. Search is case-insensitive substring match over notes, category, service line-item types, station, platform, from and to; it does not search amounts. |
| **LD-12** | Delete and Undo edges | Delete is offered on the edit screen and as a swipe on a History row. If the app is backgrounded or closed during the 5-second window the delete is committed at once. Quick successive deletes each have their own window, with Undo shown for the latest. `lastOdometer` is recomputed when the delete commits. |
| **LD-13** | Edit and delete authority | The UI follows the roles table: the owner can edit and delete any entry; a member edits and deletes only their own; other members' entries are read-only for a member. An entry's type cannot change on edit; its date can. |
| **RD-01** | Status and "overdue" | States: Upcoming, Due soon (inside the lead window), Due today, Overdue, Done. A date reminder is overdue from the day after its due date (device local). An odometer reminder is overdue when the vehicle's `lastOdometer` is at or past its due odometer; a predicted date passing alone does not make it overdue. |
| **RD-02** | Prediction details | Average per day is (largest reading minus smallest reading) divided by the days between them, over readings dated in the last 90 days by entry date, and needs at least 2 readings spanning 7 days. Readings are every entry's odometer plus trips' start and end odometers; the starting odometer counts only if its date falls in the window. Predicted due date is the date of the latest reading plus (due odometer minus latest reading) divided by the average. Mile vehicles use miles per day, with the default lead distance of 500 in the vehicle's unit. `hours` vehicles can use date reminders only; the odometer trigger is hidden for them (OQ-11). |
| **RD-03** | Notification schedule details | Notifications at 9:00 local: a lead notification `leadDays` before the due date, a due-date notification (independent of lead days), and one overdue notification the day after; none after that. A snoozed reminder has no scheduled notification until the snooze ends. A predicted odometer notification is scheduled for 9:00 on the date the predicted remaining distance equals the lead distance. With no prediction, a notification fires on the phone that logs, or later picks up, a reading within the lead distance. |
| **RD-04** | Matching details | Any matching entry completes an active reminder only if its date is on or after the reminder's creation date; every matching active reminder completes.
